CNA's Blog publishes news, notes, and technical articles about CNA, a C++
reimplementation of the XNA 4.0 API.

## How the content is organized

Each article preserves its publication date, author, categories, and tags.
Article URLs follow the `year/month/day/slug` structure so links from the
original ClassicPress blog can remain valid.

Articles are maintained as Markdown files and converted into a static website.
The publishing pipeline generates the dated archives, category pages, tag
pages, RSS feed, and sitemap automatically.
